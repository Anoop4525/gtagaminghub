# Legal, Intellectual Property & Trademark Checklist

**Site:** `https://gtagaminghub.store`

---

## 1. Brand Independence Checklist

- [x] **No Imitation Logos:** Site uses an original geometric "GG" monogram and custom vector radar elements, never copying Rockstar Games' R* logo or official GTA typography.
- [x] **Clear Independence Disclaimer:** Prominently featured on the site header, footer, about page, and legal notices:
  > *"GTA Gaming Hub is an independent gaming discovery website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Product and game names belong to their respective owners."*
- [x] **No Unlicensed Promotional Artwork:** Product visuals use licensed/original vector illustrations, icons, and CSS graphics rather than scraped proprietary screenshots or copyrighted promotional posters.
- [x] **Prominent Affiliate Disclosure:** Visible FTC and advertising disclosure:
  > *"Some links on this site are affiliate links. We may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you."*
- [x] **Accurate Product Presentation:** Never misrepresents digital code-in-box items as physical game discs.
- [x] **No Gray Market Key Resellers:** Strictly links only to verified first-party stores (PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Rockstar Store) or authorized national retailers (Amazon India).
- [x] **Age Restrictions Respected:** GTA M (17+) / PEGI 18 ratings and 18+ collectible warnings (e.g. Zippo lighter) explicitly noted.
